# Gap fixes from self-review (2026-07-18)

Founder-directed fixes for the 7 gaps found in the 2026-07-18 self-review of all SoloBooks design work.

## 1. Storage architecture (CRITICAL — design as written would not work)

Research (verified 2026-07-18): Google service accounts have a 15GB Drive quota, and **SAs created
after April 15, 2025 have ZERO Drive storage quota** — they cannot own files in My Drive at all.
Sources: developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/limits; harshalrj25.medium.com (SA 15GB
handling); n8n community reports of post-2025 SA uploads failing.

**Decision:** SoloBooks runs under a Google Workspace tenant. All company folders/files live in
**Shared Drives** (files belong to the drive, not the SA); the service account is a member with
Manager role. View-only sharing to the customer's email works unchanged. Sharding: one Shared Drive
holds many companies (400k-item cap per shared drive; a company-year ≈ 15 files → thousands of
companies per drive); provision registry stores shared_drive_id per company; new drives created as
caps approach. Domain-wide delegation rejected (impersonation complexity, broader blast radius).
Phase 0 spike must verify: shared-drive file creation by SA member, external view-only sharing,
values.append + Docs export inside shared drives.

## 2. Cash-basis recognition algorithm (canonical definition)

Cash-basis P&L = **money-movement recategorization**: every transaction touching a money account
(Bank / Credit Card / Cash) drives recognition; everything else is invisible on cash basis.

Rules:
- Direct money↔P&L documents (SalesReceipt, direct expense, owner draw/expense): recognize as-is at
  transaction date.
- Payment settling invoice(s) (money DR + AR CR with refs): recognize the applied amount **pro-rata
  across ALL credit lines of the invoice's current posted version** (income lines AND sales-tax
  line), at the PAYMENT date. Tax portion goes to sales-tax-liability-collected, never P&L.
- BillPayment mirror: recognize expense pro-rata at payment date.
- **Credit card purchases recognize at charge date** (IRS treats card charges as paid when charged) —
  credit card is a money account for recognition purposes; paying the card bill is a transfer, no P&L.
- Non-cash settlements recognize NOTHING and never will: credit notes, write-offs, discounts, AR/AP
  offsets — no cash moved, no cash-basis income/expense. (Early-pay discount: only the 980 cash is
  income; the 20 discount line never recognizes.)
- Retainers/prepayments: cash basis recognizes at RECEIPT — the CR Unearned Revenue leg is reported
  as income under a dedicated "Customer deposits received" cash-basis line. The later application
  (DR Unearned / CR AR ref) is non-cash → no second recognition.
- Refunds (DR AR ref / CR Bank): negative income pro-rata at refund date. NSF reversal: reverses the
  original recognition at reversal date.
- Transfers (money↔money): excluded, both bases.
- Sales-tax liability report on cash basis = tax portion of cash actually received.

This is THE definition `v_pnl_cash` implements; expected fixture values derive from these rules.

## 3. Dates & timezones

- Company object gains `timezone` (IANA string, set at onboarding, default America/New_York).
- All document dates are **plain calendar dates** (no time component) — the date the user states is
  the date; no timezone math ever applies to a document date. Month-file assignment = month of that
  plain date. Deterministic.
- Timezone is used ONLY to: resolve relative dates in chat ("today", "last month") and render
  `posted_at` audit timestamps (stored UTC).
- `fiscal_year_start` = "MM-DD"; v1 validates day == 01 (first-of-month fiscal years only;
  mid-month deferred with a clear error).

## 4. row_range invalidation on re-render

`re_render_month` (unlock case) invalidates every affected PostingRecord's `outbox.row_range`
(set to null + `rerendered: true`) and recomputes them from the rebuild ordering as part of the swap.
"View in sheet" links resolve lazily: null row_range → locate by txn_id search at link time.

## 5. Matching hints schema (the LLM↔engine contract)

`record_payment` (and `pay_bill`) accept a structured `hints` object — the ONLY channel from agent
extraction to the deterministic engine:
{ "invoice_refs": ["INV-0042"], "project": "Website" | null, "memo_verbatim": "<user's words>" }
Unknown/closed refs → validation error returned to the agent naming valid open refs (agent corrects;
engine never guesses). memo_verbatim is stored on the payment for audit, not parsed by the engine.

## 6. Buyer-panel framing correction

Sober reading added to findings: NO segment averaged above "might or might not" (2.65–2.91).
The concept as scoped converts one niche (AI-native spreadsheet refugees); advisor interest is
promising but is interest, not commitment. Real-buyer conversations gate serious investment —
synthetic panel results are directional only.

## 7. Exportability mechanism (the "your books are yours" promise)

Stated mechanism: view-only shared files remain copyable by the user (File → Make a copy) and the
folder remains shared after cancellation; `export_accountant_packet` provides the structured export.
Post-cancellation the folder is never deleted for a documented retention period (v1: indefinite).
