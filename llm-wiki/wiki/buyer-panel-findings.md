# Buyer Panel Findings (SSR, 15 respondents)

> Synthetic buyer panel (3 segments × 5): advisors 2.91 > consultants 2.68 ≈ creators 2.65 mean purchase intent. Trust the rankings and objection themes, not absolute scores.

**Sober framing:** no segment averaged above "might or might not." The concept as scoped converts one niche — AI-native spreadsheet refugees — and advisor interest is interest, not commitment. Real-buyer conversations gate serious investment; this panel is directional only.

**Who converts:** spreadsheet-DIY, already-pays-for-Claude solopreneurs (EV 3.7–3.8). **Who doesn't:** anyone whose incumbent automates data capture (FreshBooks/QBO/bookkeeper users, EV 1.9–2.1). **Surprise:** CPA/bookkeeper advisors scored highest — the portable locked ledger ([[general-ledger-sheet]]) is a distribution channel ("I'd just prepare the return").

**Objection themes ranked:** (1) "I am the data pipe" — no bank/CSV import, 11/15; (2) pricing absent, 15/15 reacted; (3) completeness of narrated books, 5/5 advisors; (4) missing payment links / recurring billing, 2 dealbreakers; (5) AI trust, 2.

**What resonated:** per-client/launch profitability, AR aging, portable tied-out sheets, deposits-as-unearned-revenue ([[pairing-and-matching]]), approval controls ([[approval-flow]]), the close tie-out ([[period-locking-month-close]]).

**Price bands stated:** ~$25/mo converts enthusiasts; $50+ requires full incumbent replacement; unpriced reads "expensive/not ready". Anchors: FreshBooks $19–35, QBSE $15–25, Wave free, + ~$20 Claude prerequisite.

**Scope consequences** applied in [[v1-scope]] (CSV import into v1, accountant packet, completeness nudges, price at launch; payment links + recurring to later tiers; time-tracking/mileage/receipt-OCR cuts validated).
