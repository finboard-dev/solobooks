## 3. Did batch 3's three judgment edits hold?

**R12's carrier — NO.** Broken independently by three attackers in three unrelated ways (W-4 anchor regression, W-5 unevaluable-for-bills, W-5 per-share promise), plus its "exactly one branch fires *by construction*" claim is false: mutual exclusion holds only on the prepaid debit leg, while the competing carrier (`COST_RECOVERY` on the amortization) sits on a posting authored months later by hand, with no line pairing in `gl_lines` to link them. **The idea is right and must survive** — decide at entry, carry a tag, never let the view guess a benefit period. That part four attackers confirmed is a genuine, durable fix of pass 3's W-2. **The placement is wrong**: the tag belongs on the settlement, not on the document.

**The preamble — HALF held, and it is the half that was arithmetic rather than judgment.** `Σ recognized ≤ line amount` per (`gl_line`, recognition event) survived a dedicated four-way assault — partial settlement across tax years, R4 pro-rata, R12 shares, R7 negatives, NSF-then-repayment, R5a partials, overpayment — and nobody produced `Σ > line amount` or a case the invariant wrongly forbids. **Keep it verbatim; it is the best sentence batch 3 wrote.** The *published ladder*, which was new judgment, failed on all three axes: not correctly ordered (W-2), not complete (W-3: R6 and R7 have no rung and are not declared outside), not total (W-16: typed terminal rung). Publishing the order was the right call — an unpublished order is not a definition — but what got published is a partial function with a missing rung and an inversion.

**The onboarding mechanism — the JUDGMENT held, the mechanism did not.** Deleting the OBE-substitution bullet was correct and I endorse it: all four attackers independently traced rule 5 alone through both prior-TB depths and confirmed the asset lands exactly once, the ACCRUAL branch cancels by construction, and the two mechanisms together zeroed the modal no-TB user's basis. ## 4. Is the carrier check sound?

**Accept the instrument. Reject the discharge. Add two invariants and one process rule.**

The instrument is real and it is the first thing in this project to catch a defect of this class without an adversary — it reproduced W-1, W-2 and W-5 mechanically. Keep it and run it every batch.

The discharge is not sound, and agent 4's rejection is correct on every count I checked. **Invariant 4 never passed at all** (`expect.recognized` was never written — W-12). **Invariant 2 fails in five places** (W-8, W-11 × 4). **Invariant 1 was run over a subset of its own domain** — `NSF_REVERSAL` is read by no rule (W-3), `OPENING_BALANCE` by no rule (W-20), `OriginType` has no members at all, and `DISPOSAL_FORM_4797` is unraisable (W-8). **Invariant 3 cannot be discharged statically at all** while `add_account` mints accounts at posting time and `coa_tax_line_map` lives on an append-only policy set that account creation does not version — and agent 4 discharged it against the seed list, certifying the very account (W-10) that no posting rule credits.

Four invariants are **not** sufficient. Add:

- **5th (carrier class):** every member a rule *reads* must have a **named writer**, and where the writer is a human answer, an **undeclared-intent detector**. Catches W-13; would have caught pass 3's W-2 a second way.
- **6th (closure class — the one all four structurally cannot see):** every enumerated set used as a **ladder, a gate, or a bridge** must be a **partition of R1–R12** — every rule appears as a rung/term or is named as contributing nothing. Catches W-3, W-9 and W-16 in one grep, and it is precisely pass 2's W-8 recurring untreated.
- **7th, a process rule not an invariant:** **no wiki rule edit ships without the corresponding plan task edited in the same commit.** This has now failed three passes running and produced the single highest-consequence defect in the repo (W-1).
